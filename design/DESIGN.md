# SAGA16 Design System & Tokens (from Stitch Project 6907072400396383877)

## Colors
- Dark Teal: `#091414`
- Hero Background: `#101012` with soft teal radial glow
- Off-white: `#FAF9F8` (Canvas / Light background)
- Mint: `#E5F0E1` / `#E5EFE6` (Cards / Light containers)
- Deep Teal: `#326262` (Primary brand color / Container)
- Accent Green: `#6BBF9A` (High-priority action / badge accent)
- Muted text on dark: `#A9B5B4`
- Neutral primary text on light: `#1C1B1B`
- Neutral muted text on light: `#586261`

## Typography
- Font Family: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Headings: Medium weight (500) or bold as designated, tracking -0.03em, line-height 1.1
- Body: Normal tracking 0em, line-height 1.5 - 1.75
- Logo: SAGA16 in heavy (900), italic, uppercase, wide-tracked letters (`tracking-widest italic font-black`), shown ONCE in the navbar.

## Geometry & Radius
- Pills: `rounded-full` (`9999px`) for buttons, badges, chips
- Cards: `rounded-3xl` (`24px`) radius
- Borders: 1px teal-tinted borders (`border border-[#326262]/20` or `border-[#326262]/10`)
- Max container width: `1280px` (`max-w-7xl`)
- Section padding: `80px` to `120px` desktop (`py-20` to `py-28`), `48px` to `64px` mobile (`py-12` to `py-16`).
